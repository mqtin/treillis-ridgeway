# Sources, rights and credits

This project reconstructs the argument of a book under copyright. It does not reproduce its text: it **paraphrases** it, pointing to each page, and **quotes** only short extracts. **To read Ridgeway, read Ridgeway.**

## The book

- Cecilia L. Ridgeway, *Status: Why Is It Everywhere? Why Does It Matter?*, New York, Russell Sage Foundation, 2019. © Russell Sage Foundation.
- What the repository contains of the book: short quotations (a few words, at most one sentence), each with its page, and the titles of its chapters. The text itself is not distributed: the `source/` folder is excluded from the repository, and `outils/check.py` checks that it stays so.
- These quotations fall under the right of short quotation (French Intellectual Property Code, art. L122-5, 3°): they are incorporated into a work of analysis and their source is given. They are **not** covered by the repository's licences (see `LICENSE`).

## Languages

- The reconstruction is written in French and translated into English by us. Ridgeway's words are quoted in English, as they are; in the French bodies, some are translated by us.
- To our knowledge, the book has no published French translation; the French equivalents of its concepts (schème culturel, croyances de statut, etc.) are ours.

## Other authors

The work of other authors (Weber, Goffman, Sewell, Tilly, Blumer, Fiske…) and the empirical studies are presented as Ridgeway reports them, with the page where she does so; their full references are in the book's notes.
